# SDC client demonstration

Start on the public website. The positioning is veteran-led people supported by connected operational systems. The website and portal use the user-supplied SDC eagle crest and its navy-and-yellow brand palette. The campus image is generated, illustrative photography; it is not an SDC location or employee.

## Five-minute walkthrough

1. Show the headline, company scale, service offering, technology preview and veteran leadership story.
2. Open **Client & team portal** to SDC Command. Explain that every person and site in the portal is fictional.
3. Review the three demo sites and the one unassigned active officer. Assign Suresh Naik (SDC-0104) to Northstar Hospital on 21 September 2026. Publish, then refresh: the assignment and activity trail remain.
4. Open Attendance, Incidents and Approvals. Mark a scheduled officer present, resolve a sample incident and approve a leave request.
5. Import a roster: download the CSV template, select the file and review it before publishing. Re-importing the same employee/date is rejected. No photograph OCR is represented as working.
6. Switch Preview role to Operations Manager, Site Lead and Employee. The employee view is intentionally simple and phone-friendly.
7. Export a CSV from Reports. Finish on the public website’s assessment call to action.

## What works

- Responsive public site with service details, industry tabs, technology tabs and an enquiry-email composer. The user reviews and sends the email in their mail app; the website sends nothing automatically.
- Private, authenticated demonstration workspace backed by Cloudflare D1; changes survive reloads.
- Employee creation and search; profile and duty history.
- Single assignments and reviewed CSV roster batches with server validation and conflict checks.
- Attendance, incident reporting/resolution, leave decisions and dated activity history.
- Exportable headcount, attendance, complete duty history and activity records.
- Role previews with action restrictions and site-lead mutation scoping, all inside the owner’s demo workspace.

## Production work still required

This is a client demonstration, not a replacement HR or live guarding system. The role selector is a presentation tool, not staff authentication. The site remains owner-private. Production rollout needs approved employee identities and server-owned roles/memberships, scoped read permissions, password/SSO policies, account recovery, real data migration, retention/backups, monitoring, granular normalized records, payroll rules, and operational acceptance testing. GPS/biometric attendance, CCTV/device integration, notifications, photo OCR and statutory filing are not enabled. Live employee information and the supplied shared password were not imported.

Use a separate production environment and reviewed migration plan. Do not turn this owner-only demo into a public personnel system by simply changing its sharing setting.

## Content decisions to confirm with the client

- Brochure: 600+ workforce. Pitch deck: 800+. Demo uses the conservative 600+ from the brochure and current website.
- Documents use inconsistent third phone numbers. Demo uses the consistent primary number +91 99803 43925 and sdcpvtltd01@gmail.com.
- 2019 is labelled as the PSARA licence year, not the incorporation year; the supplied CIN contains 2017.
- Client names are presented as selected organisations served according to the company profile, not current contract endorsements.
- Blockchain identity, “first agency” claims, guaranteed compliance and percentage cost savings were not repeated as verified capabilities.
- Public copy references supplied registration details, but licence validity/current workforce figures require owner confirmation before public release.
