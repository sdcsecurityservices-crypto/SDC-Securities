# What we need from SDC

To finish the website, the legal pages and the "show-off" items the big players (such as SIS India) have, we need the following from SDC. Each item is ready on the site and switches on as soon as we have the answer.

## Legal and compliance (required)
1. **Grievance Officer:** name, designation, email, phone, and office days and hours. Needed on the Privacy Policy and Grievance pages (DPDP Act 2023; IT Rules 2011).
2. **Data protection contact:** only if this is a different person from the Grievance Officer.
3. **Annual return (Form MGT-7A):** the latest filed PDF, from the company secretary or accountant (Companies Act 2013, s.92(3)).
4. **Legal review:** SDC's lawyer to review the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Grievance pages, and confirm Indian law and Bengaluru courts.
5. **Company email:** if SDC has its own domain, an address such as grievance@ or info@ on it, instead of Gmail.

## Credibility items, from the SIS India comparison
6. **Guard helpline:** the number SDC guards should call for pay, PF/ESI, uniform or problems at a post, and its hours. The site uses the office number for now.
7. **Training figures:**
   - induction length in days or hours;
   - number of guards trained so far;
   - refresher frequency;
   - academy location and size.
8. **Security equipment:** tick what SDC actually supplies or manages. Only ticked items will be shown.
   - CCTV
   - Body-worn cameras
   - Control-room monitoring
   - Boom or flap barriers
   - Visitor management
   - Biometric or card access
   - Handheld metal detectors
   - Door-frame metal detectors
   - Baggage scanners
   - Walkie-talkies
   - PA systems
   - Panic buttons
   - Extinguishers and refills
   - Fire alarm checks
   - Fire drills
   - QR checkpoints
   - Torches, batons, rain gear
9. **Milestones:** year by year since 2017, for example first client, PSARA licence, first 100 / 500 / 1000 people, ISO, new sectors and new cities.
10. **Recognition:** client appreciation letters, awards or newspaper mentions, if any.
11. **Event security:** does SDC regularly cover events? If so, the kinds of events and their size.
12. **Founder's credo:** Lokesh to pick one of these lines, or write his own:
    - "Look after the guard, and the guard will look after your site."
    - "Discipline is not a slogan. It is what happens at 3 am when nobody is watching."
    - "Every post is a promise. We keep it, shift after shift."
    - "I served the nation in uniform. Now I make sure every SDC uniform serves our clients with the same honour."

## People and content
13. **Third director:** photo (high resolution, plain background) and a short profile.
14. **Translation review:** a native speaker to check the Kannada and Hindi wording on the careers pages and the privacy summaries.
15. **Client logos:** confirmation that every client shown on the Wall of honour is happy to be named, especially Lamborghini Bangalore.
